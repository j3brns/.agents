# Causeway deck — presenter talk-track (v2: sunny-day-first)

Open `causeway.html` in any browser. Arrow keys / space to advance; `P` then browser
Print → "Save as PDF" for a handout. ~8 minutes. All visuals are inline SVG — fully
self-contained, no network, render anywhere. Energy on **acceleration**; governance is
what lets you say yes.

1. **Title — "A sunny day in August."** "Forget the architecture for a second. Let me
   describe what a good day looks like when this exists."
2. **The vignette (Maya's day).** Read it like a story. Land the punch: "she never sat in
   an approval meeting — and all of it was governed."
3. **Journey timeline (SVG).** "Here's her week on one line — idea at 9, sandbox at 9:05,
   two-key promote, attested by Thursday. The path *rises* as the work matures."
4. **The wall (SVG).** "Why is that day impossible today? This. A wall with no door —
   innovation theatre. The wall doesn't keep risk out, it keeps evidence from being
   collected."
5. **Wall → ramp (SVG).** The money picture. "Replace the wall with a ramp. Four stages,
   one repo, governance that rises only as fast as the work earns it."
6. **The ratchet (SVG).** "Conformance isn't a checkbox — it's structural. Each click up
   tightens the account's own guardrails, binding even a credentialed agent, and a pawl
   stops it sliding back. The certificate authorises each click."
7. **Two keys (SVG).** "Authority lives with the crew, not a committee. Like launch codes:
   two qualified concurrences, at least one independent of the author, at most one an
   agent. You only call shore when it's beyond your envelope."
8. **The cage (SVG).** "Why can we hand the crew the keys? Because the box is hard-walled —
   permissions by the SCP ratchet, money by the lease cap. Move fast inside; you can't
   blow out of it."
9. **Trust but verify.** For the skeptic: "self-assert to move, verify in parallel, revoke
   on failure. Durable state is always evidence, never opinion."
10. **The passport.** Turn to the CISO: "every step issues a signed, published certificate.
    Read a unit's standing without a meeting."
11. **What we built.** "Not an empire — we wrapped ISB + AgentCore + GitLab and removed one
    wall. Two things to operate."
12. **The ask.** "Delegated SCP scope over the sandbox subtree — not the org. Bless four
    spikes. One crew to go first."
13. **One breath.** Read it slowly. Stop talking.

Slides to slow down on: 3 (journey), 5 (ramp), 6 (ratchet), 7 (two keys). The SVGs are the
argument — let them sit on screen.
