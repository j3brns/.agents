# Causeway deck — presenter talk-track

Open `causeway.html` in any browser. Arrow keys / space to advance; `P` then browser
Print → "Save as PDF" for a handout. ~7 minutes at a brisk pace. Keep the energy on
**acceleration**; governance is the thing that lets you say yes, not the point.

1. **Title** — "We can give every team fast, permissive access to AI *and* keep it
   governed. The trick is to stop treating those as opposites."
2. **The mandate** — "Everyone in this room agrees with the goal. We only ever fight
   about the brake. The brake is approvals."
3. **Bottleneck** — "Weeks in a queue, minutes of work. The scarce resource isn't GPUs —
   it's permission to begin, paid again at every step."
4. **False choice** — "Move-fast and stay-safe are both offered as the answer. Both are
   wrong, because the problem isn't the controls — it's the cliff between them."
5. **Reframe** — the money slide. "Replace the wall with a ramp. Four stages, one repo,
   governance that rises only as fast as the work earns it."
6. **Progressive conformance** — "Conformance you accrue, not a gate you queue at. The
   proof is a by-product of building."
7. **Developer day one** — "One file. Three actions. Minutes to a live sandbox. This is
   the acceleration promise, made concrete."
8. **Adjudicator** — the second money slide. "You do not stop for approvals. An agent
   reads every change for risk and cost. Clear? Keep moving. Elevated? *Then* — and only
   then — two pairs of eyes. That's how we kill continuous out-of-band approvals."
9. **Trust but verify** — for the skeptic. "Self-assert to move, verify in parallel,
   revoke on failure. The durable state is always evidence, never opinion."
10. **The ladder** — "Here's what actually ramps: model freedom narrows, packaging
    hardens, scrutiny rises, controls go from advisory to preventive."
11. **Certificate** — turn to the CISO. "Every step issues a signed, published passport.
    You read a unit's standing without a meeting and without pipeline access."
12. **What we built** — "We didn't build a platform empire. We wrapped what AWS and
    GitLab already give us and removed one wall. Two things to operate."
13. **The ask** — "Delegated SCP scope over the sandbox subtree — not the org. Bless
    three spikes. Give me one BU to go first."
14. **One breath** — read it slowly, then stop talking.
