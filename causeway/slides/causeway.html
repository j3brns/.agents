The sunny day

09:00 — Maya, on the Payments crew, has an idea for a refund-triage agent.

09:05 — She files one issue. A repo and a live AWS sandbox reply with a link. No ticket. No keys to copy.

She builds with her AI pair all morning — fast, permissive, inside the inner loop.

11:30 — She promotes to Incubate: one line in a file. Two keys turn — hers and the crew’s agent — and it’s done.

Thursday — Hardened, attested, a signed certificate published. She never sat in an approval meeting.

And yet — all of it was governed

Spend bounded (preventive caps + budget backstop). Guardrails tightened at each step, binding even her agent. Every promotion signed and evidenced. The CISO can read its standing without a meeting. Speed and control, at the same time.

This is the “sunny day”. The rest of the deck is the journey that earns it — and quietly handles the rainy days too (revoke, freeze, quarantine).

Why that day is impossible today

Between the sandbox and production:
a wall.

Today a working prototype usually dies at lease end, and gets rebuilt later from memory. The wall doesn’t stop risk — it stops the evidence ever being collected.

The reframe

Replace the wall with a ramp.

The journey, made structural

Conformance is a ratchet, not a checkbox.

Authority lives with the crew — not a committee

Two keys to launch. One may be an agent.

Two qualified concurrences · at least one independent of the author (no self-approval) · at most one an agent. You only call shore when the action exceeds the crew’s envelope — the exception.

The same call, every push

Why we can hand the crew the keys

The cage licenses the freedom.

Crews get fast, two-key authority because the box is walled — you can only spend on what the SCP tier and token/rate caps allow, and a small lease budget backstops the rest. Blast radius stays bounded. (Pre-prod → advisory + FinOps.)

The safety underneath the speed

Trust, but verify — and revoke.

Self-assert

You move now. The assertion buys motion.

Verify in parallel

The verifier still runs on every transition — asynchronously.

Revoke on failure

Evidence fails → the certificate is revoked, the unit freezes.

The durable state is always evidence, not opinion. Self-assertion changes when the check falls — never whether proof is required.

For the CISO — provably governed

Every step issues a passport. Published.

A signed, human-readable certificate per unit: stage, evidence, adjudication, approvers, the verifying release. A passport, not a logfile.

Standing is visible on the docs site — an auditor reads a unit’s attestation trail without touching a pipeline, and without a meeting.

The ask

Acceleration with a paper trail.

  • ①
    Delegated SCP scope over the sandbox subtree — so conformance is preventive, landed before first Harden. (Not whole-org. Ever.)
  • ②
    Bless four spikes — harvest hold, pipeline-vended lease, one verified promotion, the SCP ratchet vs. drift. Named risks, each with a fallback.
  • ③
    One crew to go first. Minutes to sandbox; a day to onboard.
In one breath

A sandbox where nothing is trusted and everything is permitted, graduating — one self-asserted, two-key, evidence-stamped step at a time, inside a box it can’t blow out of — into an estate where nothing is permitted except what’s attested.
